South Park
This adult animated show follows the exploits of four boys in the quiet mountain town of South Park, Colorado. Stan, Kyle, Kenny, and Eric fight the forces of evil, including genetically engineered towels, crab people, man/bear/pig hybrids, TV execs, and each other. The four boys face the challenges of growing up with crazy parents, crazy friends, and crazy people. This Emmy winning series explores topics like homosexuality, death, the media, and education in every episode.
The Town
home to an assortment of other characters, including students, families, and elementary school staff.[10] Prominent settings include South Park Elementary, various neighborhoods and the surrounding mountain range, actual Colorado landmarks, and the businesses along the town's main street, all of which are based on the appearance of similar locations in Fairplay, Colorado.
The Main Characters
Stan Marsh
The everyman. Usually the one stuck being the voice of reason, and the most tired of the group.
Kyle Broflovski
Stan's best friend and the group's conscience. Quick to argue, quicker to take a moral stand.
Eric Cartman
Scheming, selfish, and the engine of most of the trouble. The group's chaos agent.
Kenny McCormick
The muffled one in the orange parka, famous for a long-running gag of dramatic exits.
